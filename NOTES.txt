Yes, that's exactly what happens if you set cfg.log.restore_path to your training checkpoint directory. The restore function in checkpoint.py:133 loads the walker positions from the training checkpoint, so your inference run starts from the already-thermalized walkers rather than from scratch.

You'd also want to skip the burn-in phase since the walkers are already thermalized:


cfg.optim.optimizer = 'none'
cfg.optim.iterations = 5000
cfg.mcmc.burn_in = 0
cfg.log.restore_path = './dipole-19e-rep1-pbc'
cfg.log.save_path = './dipole-19e-rep1-pbc'  #
